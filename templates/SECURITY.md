Thank you for helping keep <app name> and its users safe.

## Report a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately. Do not open a public issue.

- **Preferred:** use this repository's **Report a vulnerability** button (Security tab).
- **Email:** <security contact address>

Include:

- what you found and where (URL, endpoint, or file)
- the steps to reproduce it
- what an attacker could do with it

Please do not include other people's personal data. A description or a redacted sample is enough.

## What to expect

| Step | Within |
|---|---|
| We confirm we received your report | <3> working days |
| We tell you whether we accept it and our plan | <10> working days |
| We fix it, or explain why not | <30> days, depending on severity |

We will credit you when the fix ships, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Rules for testing

- Test only with accounts and data you own.
- Do not access, change, or delete other users' data. Stop as soon as you can show the problem.
- Do not run denial-of-service, spam, or social engineering against us, our users, or our providers.
- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you share it publicly.

We will not take action against research that follows these rules in good faith.

## In scope

- <production URL>
- This repository's code

## Out of scope

- Third-party services we use (report those to the provider)
- Missing best-practice headers with no demonstrated impact
- Findings from automated scanners with no proof of impact

## Supported versions

Only the current production version receives security fixes.
